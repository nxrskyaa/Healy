# Healy — a quiet little world

A painted valley you can walk through. Grass to the horizon, rain, a steam
train that crosses the north side every minute or so, a Balinese temple
gate astride the footpath with a meru tower by the pond, penjor bowing over
the way, strings of paper lanterns that come alive at dusk, and a handful
of small animals that will never tell you to hurry up.

Everything is generated by code. There is not a single image, model or audio
file in this repository — the terrain, the grass, the trees, the train, the
creatures, the sky and the score are all built at load time from noise
functions and Web Audio oscillators.

## The grass

The meadow is the centre of the piece, so it gets its own engine
(`js/world/grass.js`). Roughly 1.3 million blades live entirely on the GPU:

- **Three overlapping rings** carry blades from underfoot to the far ridge.
  They exist only to switch tessellation and thickness — density itself is one
  continuous law, `blades/m² = B · min(1, (dn/d)^1.5)`, with `B · dn^1.5` held
  constant between rings so there is no visible step anywhere.
- The exponent is **1.5 exactly** so the shader can evaluate it as
  `x·x·inversesqrt(x)` — three instructions instead of a `pow`, on every blade,
  every frame.
- Each ring's footprint is a **box that wraps around the camera**, so blades
  are placed once at load and never touched by the CPU again. Their roots read
  height and a grass mask from a **terrain lookup texture**, bilinearly filtered
  in the vertex shader.
- Every blade is a **quadratic Bézier** whose tip is solved for the quasi-static
  balance of stiffness, gravity and wind, then left ringing at its own
  frequency after a gust front passes.
- Blade width has an **angular floor**, so distant grass thins in count but
  never in coverage — which is what stops the far field from shimmering.
- A **lateral view-cone rejection** in the first five instructions of the vertex
  shader discards the half of every ring that is behind your head.

Lighting is a painter's model rather than a physical one: half-lambert wrap so a
low sun does not drop the valley into dusk, subsurface transmission through the
blade, a backlight rim along the bent-over tips, and drifting cloud shadows.

## Everything else

- `js/world/avatar.js` — the parametric character. The face is drawn into a
  512² canvas (irises with a gradient ramp, heavy upper lash line, two
  highlights) and wrapped onto the front of a chinned ovoid skull as an
  alpha-tested decal — that one texture carries the whole read. Hair is
  built from tapered shards and swept tubes; nine styles, six outfits, and
  around a hundred thousand combinations in total.
- `js/creator.js` — the character creator: its own small renderer, so you can
  make someone before the world is paid for
- `js/world/bali.js` — the temple set: candi bentar split gate, five-tiered
  meru, bale pavilion, penjor poles, lantern strings
- `js/world/trees.js` — trunks swept along curves, canopies built from
  scalloped clumps on a four-green mosaic, lit with a three-band painterly ramp
- `js/world/train.js` — the railway: a smoothed grade on a low embankment,
  instanced rails and sleepers, a steam loco with coaches, pooled smoke, and
  windows that light up after dark
- `js/world/grade.js` — the film print: custom tonemap, shadows pulled violet
  and highlights cream, a lift so nothing is pure black, light shafts when the
  sun is in frame, paper grain, warm-dark vignette, ordered dither
- `js/world/sky.js` — sky dome shader, day/night palette, sun and moon, stars,
  procedural cumulus, distant ridgelines
- `js/world/terrain.js` — the height field everything is built on, the pond, its
  water shader, flowers, stones, lily pads
- `js/world/animals.js` — soot sprites, cats, rabbits, deer, birds, fish, frogs
  and kodama, each built from primitives and given a small wandering brain
- `js/world/rain.js` — GPU rain streaks in a camera-following box, pooled
  ground splashes
- `js/audio/lofi.js` — all synthesised: a valley-sized convolution reverb,
  three-band wind, layered rain with individually scheduled droplet ticks, a
  sparse pentatonic bell score, birdsong, crickets, footsteps, and the train's
  chuff and whistle

## Running it

It is a static site with no build step. Serve the folder over HTTP (ES modules
will not load from `file://`):

```bash
npx serve
```
